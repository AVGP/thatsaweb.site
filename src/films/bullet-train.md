When I saw the trailer, I wasn't sure if it's gonna be one of these films where the trailer has all the good stuff and the rest of the film is a boring bunch of nothing.
Turns out - that's absolutely not what this film is.

It starts off relatively slow, like the train in the movie while it's pulling out of the first station, but gains speed throughout the film.
I really liked that, as it allows the film to introduce a few characters to get things rolling.

At first I thought we're in for a relatively mindless action comedy with some hilarious characters involved, but I slowly realised that this is in fact a web of storylines, loosely interwoven.
And again the film surprised me as throughout the film it becomes clear that this connection isn't as loose as it seemed.

Filled with beautiful cinematography, interesting characters and good storytelling, the film kept me entertained and surprised right until the end. Definitely a great watch!
