I have put off watching this film for a really long time. I knew a lot of people really like it, but I gotta be in the right mood to watch psychological horror. It was worth watching it, tho. I shouldn't have waited this long.

## Good old thrill

I really enjoy that the atmosphere throughout the film is a bit tense. We're joining an FBI trainee running through the forest.

While she's waiting we're confronted with a few very graphic photos hanging on a wall. That made me wonder if I watched a slasher film without realizing. That was part of my original hesitation.

But to my pleasant surprise, it wasn't the case. She meets Dr. Hannibal Lecter in a high-security institution. What an impressive antagonist!

We also learn that he approaches her differently than others and the two start a dialogue. Lecter is in control at all times. He toys with our protagonist by posing puzzles. He also forces her to disclose personal details in the conversation. But over time, they found respect for each other as the two interact.

Through this dialog, we also learn our protagonist's intrinsic motivation. She's trying to "silence the lambs" she couldn't protect when she was a child.

We also get to see our main antagonist. There's a lot of symbolism around metamorphosis and his goal to change who he is. But it's not the heavy-handed kind I dread in other movies. It gives a believable motivation and a consistent underpinning for everything they do. This is also a fundamental criticism. There are already strong stereotypes around gender identity and the associated troubles. Demme, the director, defends this as showing "tormented man". But still, remembering what "Jaws" did to sharks, I'm sceptical about this.

I like how the atmosphere of the movie builds tension through the pacing. The color grading, the switch of perspectives and use of camera angles and cuts all add to that.

It's not a cheap jump-scare thrill, this is atmospheric, classic horror through anticipation.

## The cinematic choices

I like how a lot of the movie uses dark and muted colors and switches between perspectives.

It tells us a story through the eyes of our protagonist sometimes. Other times it follows the erratic movements of the antagonist. 

## I wish I had seen this earlier

Yes, there's a bit of gore and blood, but mostly the film tells its story through psychology.

I really like movies like this, so I wish I hadn't waited this long. But I'm happy I had a look this week and it made it to the list of the top films I've seen, easily.
