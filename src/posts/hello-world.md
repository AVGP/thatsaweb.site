Hello, and welcome to this new web log! 

This site is a creative experiment designed to capture the tactile feel, vibrant colors, and unique imperfections of **Risograph (Riso) printing**, combined with a nostalgic **90s brutalist web layout**.

## Why Risograph?

Risograph is a printing process developed in Japan in the 1980s. It works similarly to screen printing, using stencils and liquid ink layered onto paper one color at a time. This process is famous for:

- **Slight misregistration**: Since each ink layer is passed through the printer separately, colors rarely align perfectly.
- **Grain and texture**: Riso inks are soy-based and dry matte, interacting with paper fibers to produce a subtle, beautiful texture.
- **Vibrant spot colors**: Fluorescent pinks, bright teals, and deep blues that are hard to replicate on digital screens.

This blog attempts to bring those characteristics onto your screen using a grain overlay, heavy flat-offset shadows, and traditional ink colors.

## Creating New Posts

Creating a post on this blog is incredibly easy! Since we've pre-configured the directory structures, you don't need any special HTML or complex templates. Just drop a new `.md` file into the `src/posts/` folder with some front-matter details at the top:

```markdown
---
title: My Cool Title
date: 2026-07-17
description: What this post is about.
tags:
  - tag1
  - tag2
---
Your markdown content goes here!
```

That's all it takes!
