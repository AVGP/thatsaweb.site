Visiting **Tokyo** is like stepping into a parallel timeline where the past and the future live side by side.

## Highlights of the Journey

- **Shibuya Crossing**: The famous scramble intersection that feels like the heartbeat of the modern city.
- **Meiji Shrine**: A peaceful forest oasis hidden right in the middle of bustling Shibuya.
- **Akihabara**: The historic home of electronics and gaming culture, filled with retro arcades and neon signs.

> "Tokyo is too much, too fast, too loud, and yet, completely peaceful."

I highly recommend visiting if you love neon lights, incredible food, and deep cultural experiences.
